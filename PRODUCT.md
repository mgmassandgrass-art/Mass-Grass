# Mass & Grass

## What it is
The shop of Farah Al-Helou, a painter from Gaza. She paints in watercolour on paper; the shop sells her paintings as
digital files (PDF/PNG to print at home), postcards, die-cut stickers and a yearly wall calendar, and prints some pieces
to order and ships them to the United Arab Emirates and Denmark. Every sale supports her to keep painting.

## Who it is for
People abroad who care about Palestine and about handmade art: Arabic and English speakers, mostly on phones, arriving
from Instagram. They want to see the paintings large, feel the hand that made them, and buy or share one in a minute.

## Voice
Standard Arabic (simple فصحى) and plain English. Short. Plural imperatives. No instructions, no hype. Farah's story is
told in her first person. Western digits in both languages.

## Brand commitments
- The logo: MASS& / GRASS lettering (`assets/brand/logo.svg`), ink brown.
- The paintings are the heroes; real photographs for objects (mockups), never drawn imitations of real things.
- Hand-made feeling: this is hand painting, the site should feel painted and written by hand, yet modern.
- Bilingual, RTL first.

## Surfaces
Home, shop, paintings, postcards, stickers, calendar, story, account, checkout, help, terms (Persuade / Experience);
the admin "دفتر المتجر" (Operate).

## Constraints
Static pages + a Cloudflare Worker; strict CSP (no inline scripts); vanilla JS; pages built by `tools/build-web.mjs`.

Farah’s map

My story on the map of Gaza, from al-Jalaa to a small office.

Our home in al-Jalaa

01al-Jalaa, Gaza

I’m Farah, from Gaza. I grew up in our home in al-Jalaa.

Where I started drawing

02At fourteen

That is where I started drawing when I was fourteen. Watercolour was my language from the start.

A calendar every year

03Every year

Over the years the colours became a small business: every year I designed and painted a calendar, sold at first in a shop, then directly to the people who loved my drawings.

Then the war came

042023

In 2023 we were displaced from our home in al-Jalaa to az-Zawayda.

My sketchbook came with me

05az-Zawayda

With every move from one place to another, my sketchbook came with me. I photographed the places I passed through and the scenes I didn’t want to forget, then painted them.

I won’t give up the calendar

06az-Zawayda

Between my studies and my little time, I made one decision: I would not give up the calendar. That year’s calendar became a record of every place I was displaced to.

The drawings travelled far

07From Gaza to the world

When I shared it with my drawings, it reached people in many countries, my work was shown in several exhibitions, and it touched people’s hearts.

Back home

082025

In 2025 we went back to our home in al-Jalaa.

A small office by al-Saraya

092025, al-Saraya

In 2025 I found a small office by al-Saraya in Gaza. It became the place where I put in order all the ideas in my head, and where I began to dream of something bigger.

Then we were displaced again

10Displaced again

Then we were displaced again, this time to Deir al-Balah.

A bombed house

11Coming back

Then we came back, and found our home in al-Jalaa bombed.

A rented house

12Tel al-Hawa, Gaza

We had to rent a house in Tel al-Hawa, in Gaza.

A dream since childhood

13The dream

That’s when I decided to fulfil the dream I’ve carried since childhood: a shop of my own.

Here Mass & Grass began

14The office in Gaza

I went back to the office by al-Saraya in Gaza and began building MASS&GRASS.

Massthe stone
Grassthe green

Stone and green

The name comes from mass and grass: stone and greenery. It is our heritage and the life we knew in Palestine: simple, rooted, made of stone, soil and small things growing between them.

MASS&GRASS is here to tell everything we have lived, and everything we will live.
